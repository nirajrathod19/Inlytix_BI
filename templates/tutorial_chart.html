How to Build Your First Chart

Visualizing your data is the core of Etlytix BI. Here's how to create your first chart.

Step 1: Load Your Data

First, either upload a new file or load an existing project. This will take you to the Chart Builder page.

Step 2: Select Chart Type

At the top of the Chart Builder, choose the type of chart you want to create (e.g., Bar, Line, Pie).

Step 3: Choose Your Axes

Select the columns from your dataset that you want to use for the X-Axis (categories) and Y-Axis (values).

Step 4: Generate

Click the "Generate Chart" button. Your visualization will appear instantly, along with automatically generated insights.
