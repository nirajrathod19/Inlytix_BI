Connecting to a SQL Database

Connect directly to your SQL databases to analyze data in real-time.

Step 1: Go to the Connect Page

From the dashboard, click on the "Connect Data Source" quick action.

Step 2: Enter Credentials

Fill in your database credentials, including the host, database name, username, and password. Our connections are secure and encrypted.

Step 3: Write Your Query

Write the SQL query you want to run to fetch the data for analysis. You can also use our AI-powered "Generate SQL" feature to help you write queries using natural language.

Step 4: Fetch Data

Click "Fetch Data". The results of your query will be loaded into the application, and you'll be taken to the Chart Builder to start your analysis.
