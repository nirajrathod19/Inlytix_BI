How to Import from Excel

Importing data from Excel is a straightforward process in Etlytix BI.

Step 1: Prepare Your Excel File

Ensure your Excel file (.xlsx or .xls) has a clean, tabular format. The first row should contain the headers for your columns.

Step 2: Use the Upload Page

Navigate to the "Upload" page from the dashboard's Quick Actions. Click the "Choose File" button and select your Excel file.

Step 3: Begin Analysis

Once uploaded, the system will automatically parse your file, and you will be taken to the "Prepare Data" screen to begin your analysis.
